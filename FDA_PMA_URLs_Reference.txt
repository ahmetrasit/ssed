REGULATORY NOTES
================================================================================

CLASS RECLASSIFICATION PROPOSAL:
In November 2025, FDA proposed reclassifying oncology nucleic acid-based test
systems (product codes OWD, PJG, PQP, SFL) from Class III to Class II.
Comments due: January 26, 2026
Federal Register: 90 FR 94356

This reclassification would change the regulatory pathway from PMA (Premarket
Approval) to 510(k) (Premarket Notification), potentially accelerating future
device approvals while maintaining safety and effectiveness standards.

NOTES FOR REGULATORY PROFESSIONALS
================================================================================

1. PMA numbers beginning with "P" followed by 6 digits indicate original PMAs
2. Supplements are designated with "/S" followed by 3 digits (e.g., P170019/S029)
3. De Novo submissions use "DEN" prefix (e.g., DEN170058)
4. 510(k) submissions use "K" prefix (e.g., K213883)

5. SSED documents (Summary of Safety and Effectiveness Data) are available for
   PMA-approved devices

6. 510(k) Summary documents are available for 510(k)-cleared devices

7. To find all supplements for a specific PMA, search the FDA PMA database:
   https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id={PMA_NUMBER}
