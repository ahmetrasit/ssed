NOTES AND OBSERVATIONS
================================================================================

1. Supplement Distribution:
   - FoundationOne CDx (P170019) has the most supplements: 53
   - Oncomine Dx Target (P160045) has 43 supplements
   - Combined, these two devices account for 96 of the 191 total supplements

2. Missing Devices:
   - P200006 and P200016 device names need verification
   - These may be recent approvals or transfers from different product codes

3. SSED Document Availability:
   - Not all supplements have publicly available SSED documents
   - Typically only major supplements (new indications, significant changes)
     receive published SSEDs

4. Regulatory Trend:
   - High supplement counts indicate active companion diagnostic development
   - Each supplement typically represents a new drug-CDx pairing approval

5. Product Code Reclassification:
   - FDA proposed reclassifying PQP devices from Class III to Class II
   - Proposed rule published November 2025
   - Would significantly streamline approval pathway for NGS CDx devices
