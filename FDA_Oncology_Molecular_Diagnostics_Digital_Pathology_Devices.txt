1. FOUNDATIONONE CDx (F1CDx)
   Manufacturer: Foundation Medicine, Inc., Cambridge, MA (Roche subsidiary)

   Original Approval:
   - PMA Number: P170019
   - Date: November 30, 2017
   - Device Class: III
   - Regulatory Pathway: PMA (Premarket Approval)
   - Breakthrough Device Status: June 15, 2016

   Device Description:
   Next-generation sequencing oncology panel for somatic or germline variant
   detection. Detects genetic mutations in 324 genes and two genomic signatures
   in any solid tumor type. Comprehensive genomic profiling assay using DNA
   from formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded (FFPE) tumor tissue.

   Indications:
   Companion diagnostic for multiple cancer types including non-small cell lung
   cancer, melanoma, breast cancer, colorectal cancer, cholangiocarcinoma,
   prostate cancer, ovarian cancer, and solid tumors with specific biomarkers.

   Links:

2. ONCOMINE DX TARGET TEST
   Manufacturer: Thermo Fisher Scientific (Life Technologies Corporation)

   Original Approval:
   - PMA Number: P160045
   - Date: 2017 (First FDA-approved targeted NGS-based companion diagnostic)
   - Device Class: III
   - Regulatory Pathway: PMA

   Device Description:
   Targeted next-generation sequencing-based companion diagnostic test for
   non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Detects genetic variations in BRAF,
   EGFR, and IDH1 genes and chromosome abnormalities (translocations) in ROS1
   and RET genes. Uses Ion Torrent technology.

   Indications:
   Originally approved for NSCLC, expanded to include cholangiocarcinoma, brain
   tumors (astrocytoma and oligodendroglioma), and thyroid cancer.

   Links:
   - Original SSED: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf16/p160045b.pdf
   - FDA PMA Page: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=p160045

   SUPPLEMENTS (Listed as Individual Items):

      - FDA PMA Page: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=p160045s031

3. TRUSIGHT ONCOLOGY COMPREHENSIVE (TSO Comprehensive)
   Manufacturer: Illumina, Inc.

   Original Approval:
   - PMA Number: P230011
   - Date: August 2024
   - Device Class: III
   - Regulatory Pathway: PMA
   - Breakthrough Device Designation: Yes

   Device Description:
   First FDA-approved distributable comprehensive genomic profiling in vitro
   diagnostic kit with pan-cancer companion diagnostic claims. Targeted NGS
   panel interrogating 517 genes using NextSeq 550Dx instrument. Detects SNVs,
   MNVs, insertions, deletions from DNA; fusions in 24 genes and splice
   variants from RNA. Reports Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) score.

   Indications:
   Pan-cancer comprehensive genomic profiling for solid malignant neoplasms.
   Two companion diagnostic claims:
   - NTRK gene fusions for larotrectinib (Vitrakvi)
   - RET fusion-positive NSCLC for selpercatinib (Retevmo)

   Links:
   - Technical Information: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf23/P230011C.pdf
   - Product Website: https://www.illumina.com/products/by-type/clinical-research-products/trusight-oncology-500.html

6. COBAS EGFR MUTATION TEST v2
   Manufacturer: Roche Molecular Systems, Inc.

   Original Approval:
   - PMA Number: P150044
   - Date: June 1, 2016
   - Device Class: III
   - Regulatory Pathway: PMA (Priority Review)

   Device Description:
   Real-time PCR test for qualitative detection of defined mutations in exons
   18, 19, 20, and 21 of the EGFR gene in NSCLC patients. Detects 42 defined
   mutations including L858R, exon 19 deletions, and T790M mutations. Uses
   FFPE tissue samples.

   Indications:
   Companion diagnostic for EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs) including:
   - Gefitinib (IRESSA) for first-line treatment
   - Multiple EGFR TKIs for NSCLC

   First companion diagnostic to receive therapeutic class labeling via
   180-day labeling supplement.

   Links:
   - FDA PMA Page: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P150044
   - FDA Approval: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/cobas-egfr-mutation-test-v2

NOTES FOR REGULATORY PROFESSIONALS
================================================================================

PMA SUPPLEMENTS:
This document lists major PMA supplements as individual items. However, many
devices (especially FoundationOne CDx and Guardant360 CDx) have numerous
supplements. For complete supplement history, consult the FDA PMA database
directly using the P-numbers provided.

SSED AVAILABILITY:
Summary of Safety and Effectiveness Data (SSED) documents are publicly
available for PMA-approved devices through the FDA website. Links are provided
where available. For 510(k)-cleared devices, 510(k) summaries are available
instead of SSEDs.

DEVICE CLASSIFICATION:
- Class III devices (highest risk): Require PMA approval with extensive
  clinical data. Most companion diagnostics and NGS panels fall into this
  category.
- Class II devices (moderate risk): Most clear through 510(k) pathway
  demonstrating substantial equivalence to predicate device. Digital pathology
  systems typically Class II.
- De Novo pathway: For novel devices with no existing predicate, deemed low-
  moderate risk. Establishes new device classification.

COMPANION DIAGNOSTIC LABELING:
Companion diagnostics approved since 2020 may have therapeutic class labeling
rather than single drug labeling, allowing broader utility across drug classes.

LIQUID BIOPSY EVOLUTION:
The field has rapidly evolved from tissue-only diagnostics to include
circulating tumor DNA analysis. Both FoundationOne Liquid CDx and Guardant360
CDx represent major advances enabling non-invasive comprehensive genomic
profiling.

DIGITAL PATHOLOGY REGULATORY PATHWAY:
Philips IntelliSite's 2017 De Novo clearance established the regulatory
framework, enabling subsequent digital pathology systems to use the 510(k)
pathway with IntelliSite as predicate.

AI IN DIGITAL PATHOLOGY:
Paige Prostate's 2021 De Novo authorization similarly established the pathway
for AI-based digital pathology products, enabling subsequent AI products
(like Ibex Prostate Detect) to use 510(k) pathway.
